1. Introduction & Scope

These Community Guidelines explain how to participate safely and respectfully on Aspire 101, a search-first matching layer for finding help, collaborators, and local opportunities. They apply to all activity on the site and any related communications.

2. Community Principles

  • Clarity over noise — say what you need; include location, timing, and constraints.
  • Safety first — protect yourself and others; use reporting tools when needed.
  • Respect & inclusion — welcome diverse backgrounds and viewpoints.
  • Trust by default, verify — be honest; verify when stakes are higher.
  • Privacy-minded — share only what’s required to coordinate.

3. What’s Encouraged

  • Asking for help, study groups, tutoring, project teams, ride shares, moving help, campus or neighborhood tasks.
  • Clear budgets or expectations (e.g., paid, volunteer, study swap).
  • Constructive feedback, accessibility notes, and bug reports.

4. Not Allowed on Aspire 101

We remove content and may restrict accounts for:

  • Harassment, hate, threats, sexual or explicit content, or doxxing.
  • Fraud, scams, impersonation, or deceptive/misleading posts.
  • Illegal or dangerous activities, weapons, drugs, or regulated goods.
  • Paid work targeting minors, or any request that exploits vulnerable people.
  • Spam, mass promotion, or irrelevant link dumps.
  • Cheating or contract work that violates academic or test policies (see Section 10).
Tip: If you’re unsure whether something is allowed, email team@aspires101.com before posting.

5. Safety, Reporting & Enforcement

  • Use Report on posts or profiles to alert moderators. You can also block users.
  • Share only necessary details; avoid sending sensitive info (IDs, full addresses) until trust is established.
  • We may investigate, hide, or remove content; repeat or severe violations can lead to suspensions or bans.

6. Identity & Verification

  • You must provide accurate basic info. School emails (.edu) may be tagged for easier verification.
  • No impersonation or fake organizations. Represent affiliations truthfully.

7. Posting & Matching Etiquette

  • One clear request per post. Use tags and city/location properly.
  • Respond promptly, be courteous, and close the loop (mark done, delete stale posts).
  • Share constraints up front: time, pay, skills, equipment, transport, accessibility needs.

8. Payments & Logistics

Aspire 101 is a lightweight matching layer. We do not process payments or handle logistics. If you agree to pay for a task, choose a safe method, confirm the scope in writing, and keep records. Avoid prepayment to unknown parties.

9. Youth & Sensitive Topics

  • Users must meet the age of digital consent in their region (typically 16+).
  • Do not target minors with paid work or adult themes. Report suspected endangerment immediately.

10. Academic Integrity

Collaboration is great; cheating is not. Requests that violate course policies (e.g., writing your paper or completing graded work) are prohibited. Tutoring, study groups, and concept help are welcome.

11. In-Person Meetings & Events

  • Meet in public, well-lit places; share plans with a friend. For rideshares, exchange names/plates and confirm route.
  • Follow campus rules and local laws. Event hosts are responsible for safety and accessibility.

12. Moderation Actions

Depending on severity and history, actions may include: content edits or removal, warnings, temporary limits, suspension, or permanent ban. We may also restrict features (e.g., posting or messaging) to reduce harm or spam.

13. Appeals

If you believe we made a mistake, contact us with context and any evidence. We’ll review and respond as resources allow.

14. Changes to These Guidelines

We may update these rules as the product and community evolve. Material changes will be reflected by the “Last updated” date above.

15. Contact Us

Questions or reports? Email team@aspires101.com. For emergencies or imminent harm, contact local authorities first.
