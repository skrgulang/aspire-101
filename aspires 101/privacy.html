1. Introduction & Scope

Aspire 101 (“we,” “us,” or the “Platform”) is a search-first matching layer for help and collaboration. This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share information when you use our website/app and related services. It works alongside our Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

Data controller: Aspire 101 (operating entity). Contact: legal@aspires101.com.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account details: Name/nickname, email, school/organization, city, avatar; password hashes (never plain text).
  • Platform activity: Posts/matches, likes/saves, messages, reports you file, and related metadata.
  • Support & suggestions: Items you submit (type, subject, details, page URL, optional email). For collaboration proposals, we may also collect company/organization, contact, and website links.
  • Join requests: Name, school, email, contact, notes, and any resume you upload. Files are kept in private storage; we may create time-limited signed links for review.
  • Technical logs: Device/browser info, language, IP address, timestamps, diagnostics (e.g., crash logs).
  • Partner data (optional): If you engage with campus/partner programs, we share/receive minimal data to coordinate with your direction/consent.

3. How We Use Information

  • Provide and improve core features (registration, profiles, search/matching, messaging, notifications).
  • Operate support workflows (triage feedback, review collaboration proposals, follow up as needed).
  • Process and review join requests, including secure file handling via signed URLs.
  • Protect safety and integrity (abuse prevention, spam controls, investigating reports).
  • Analytics to improve usability/performance using de-identified or aggregated data where possible.
  • Comply with law and enforce our Terms and Community Guidelines.

4. Lawful Bases for Processing (GDPR Reference)

  • Contract necessity — to deliver requested services.
  • Legitimate interests — safety, fraud prevention, and service improvement balanced against your rights.
  • Consent — where required (e.g., optional communications, certain cookies); you may withdraw anytime.
  • Legal obligations — compliance with applicable laws and lawful requests.

5. Sharing with Third Parties

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • Service providers: Hosting, databases, storage, email, analytics, and error logging (e.g., Supabase for database/storage/edge functions).
  • Chosen integrations: If you opt into campus or partner programs, we share only what’s necessary to coordinate.
  • Legal & safety: Disclosures to comply with law, enforce terms, or protect users and the Platform.
  • Business changes: In a merger or reorganization, information may transfer with comparable protections.

6. Cookies & Similar Technologies

We use cookies/local storage for session continuity, preferences, performance, and safety. Browser settings let you control cookies; disabling some may affect functionality.

7. International Transfers

Your information may be processed in countries different from where you live. Where required, we rely on lawful transfer mechanisms and apply data minimization.

8. Security

  • Role-based access and least-privilege practices.
  • Row-Level Security (RLS) and policy-based access in our database.
  • Private storage buckets for uploads; signed URLs for temporary viewing.
  • Encryption in transit; encryption at rest via our providers.

No system is perfectly secure. Use strong passwords and be cautious with sensitive information.

9. Retention

  • Account data: Retained while your account is active; deleted or anonymized within a reasonable period after deletion unless required otherwise.
  • Join requests & resumes: Typically up to 24 months for evaluation and records, unless you ask us to delete sooner.
  • Support & collaboration submissions: Typically up to 24 months for tracking and follow-ups.
  • Logs: Kept briefly for diagnostics/security unless needed for investigations.

10. Your Rights

  • Access, correct, or delete your information.
  • Withdraw consent where applicable.
  • Where available, object to or restrict processing and request data portability.
  • Complain to your supervisory authority.

We may verify your identity before acting on requests to protect you and other users.

11. Children

Services are intended for users who meet the age of digital consent in their region (typically 16+). If you believe a minor’s data was collected without proper authorization, contact us immediately.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as our services evolve. Material changes will be communicated via in-product notice, email, or other prominent means. Continuing to use the service after changes means you accept the updated policy.

13. Contact Us

Email: legal@aspires101.com

For emergencies or imminent harm, contact local authorities first.

14. Jurisdiction-Specific Notices (e.g., GDPR/CCPA)

14.1 GDPR (EU/UK)

Where GDPR applies, Aspire 101 (operating entity) is the data controller. We may rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or other safeguards for cross-border transfers where required. You may contact your local data protection authority.

14.2 CCPA/CPRA (California)

We do not sell or “share” personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. California residents can request access, deletion, or correction of personal information.

This document is provided for transparency and does not replace legal advice.
