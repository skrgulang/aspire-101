# Privacy Policy refresh requirements

This is **not** publish-ready legal text. It is an internal checklist for a counsel-reviewed replacement.

The next Privacy Policy should accurately describe the product that actually exists.

## Controller / brand

Use the finalized legal formulation for:

- Cloudora Labs, Inc.
- Aspire 101 assumed business name / product
- legal/privacy contact address

## Data collected

The policy should address, where applicable:

- account / profile information;
- campus / school verification;
- phone verification;
- identity-verification status / provider references;
- requests, responses, marketplace listings, connections, reviews;
- messages;
- saved / draft content and media;
- payment / fee / refund / dispute / payout metadata;
- delivery addresses / instructions;
- shipping / tracking metadata;
- temporary location sharing;
- safety reports / support / Resolution Center evidence;
- device/browser/log data;
- product analytics;
- transactional-email delivery metadata.

## Purpose

Describe operational purposes such as:

- account authentication;
- campus trust / verification;
- discovery / matching;
- marketplace transactions;
- payment protection;
- seller/provider onboarding;
- fulfillment / delivery;
- safety / moderation;
- fraud / abuse prevention;
- customer support;
- product analytics;
- reliability / security monitoring;
- legal / accounting compliance.

## Service providers

The current product uses providers that may process data, including:

- Supabase
- Vercel
- Stripe / Stripe Connect / identity-related Stripe flows
- Resend
- Datadog
- Amplitude
- shipping providers / Shippo-related flows where used

The final policy should describe categories and/or named providers in the form counsel recommends.

## Precise / sensitive data

The final policy should be explicit about:

- temporary precise location sharing, if enabled by the user;
- delivery address data;
- identity-verification data;
- payment / transaction data;
- safety / dispute information.

## Retention

Do not promise blanket immediate deletion.

Define category-specific retention, including:

- account / profile;
- payment / accounting;
- fraud / dispute;
- safety / moderation;
- messages;
- delivery data;
- identity verification;
- analytics / logs;
- support;
- uploaded drafts / media.

## Rights / deletion

Reflect the real self-service account deletion behavior, including:

- blockers for active transactions / disputes;
- deletion / anonymization of direct account data;
- preservation of records when required for counterpart integrity, safety, financial, legal, fraud, or dispute purposes.

## Age / eligibility

Reconcile the current contradiction:

- Terms: 18+
- Privacy: “typically 16+” digital-consent language

This requires a product/legal decision.

## Analytics / advertising language

Current product analytics are used for product improvement / reliability.

Do not claim behavioral advertising, sale of personal data, or cross-context advertising unless actually implemented and reviewed.

## Publication process

1. Founder resolves business-policy decisions.
2. Counsel reviews full Terms + Privacy + marketplace / payment language together.
3. Update pages in one controlled release.
4. Update “last updated” date.
5. Preserve prior version internally.
6. Announce material changes as required.
