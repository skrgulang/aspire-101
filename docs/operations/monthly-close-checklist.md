# Cloudora Labs / Aspire 101 monthly close checklist

This checklist is an operating baseline, not accounting or tax advice. Use a qualified accountant/tax professional for filing positions, revenue recognition, sales-tax obligations, contractor reporting, and other jurisdiction-specific decisions.

## Month-end source records

Collect and retain:

- business-bank statement;
- Stripe balance/payout/fee/refund/dispute reports;
- Aspire payment and platform-fee ledger totals;
- Shippo/shipping charges;
- SaaS/provider invoices;
- founder contributions, reimbursements, and owner-paid expenses;
- receipts for business purchases;
- payroll/contractor records if applicable.

## Reconciliation sequence

1. **Bank:** beginning balance + inflows - outflows = ending statement balance.
2. **Stripe:** gross customer collections - Stripe fees - refunds/disputes - transfers/payout effects = provider balance movement.
3. **Aspire ledger:** compare transaction/payment/order state to Stripe provider state; investigate differences rather than forcing a balancing entry.
4. **Platform revenue:** separate Aspire platform fees from pass-through seller/provider amounts.
5. **Shipping:** reconcile Shippo/label charges to the related orders.
6. **Expenses:** classify recurring SaaS, legal, marketing, insurance, hosting, email, and other operating spend.
7. **Founder money:** distinguish capital contribution, reimbursement, shareholder/owner loan, and company expense; do not leave personal/company transfers uncategorized.
8. **Open items:** list unreconciled payments, refunds, failed payouts, chargebacks, and vendor credits.

## Monthly outputs

- Profit & loss.
- Balance sheet.
- Cash balance and 3-month cash trend.
- Platform GMV and net platform revenue.
- Stripe fees/refunds/disputes.
- Shipping costs.
- SaaS burn.
- Accounts payable / reimbursements due.
- One-page exception list for anything that does not reconcile.

## Recordkeeping

Keep final monthly statements/reports and the reconciliation workbook in the company-controlled drive. Avoid storing bank credentials, full card numbers, or raw API secrets in bookkeeping files.

## Cadence

- Days 1–3: collect statements/provider exports.
- Days 3–5: reconcile bank, Stripe, shipping, and expenses.
- By day 7: finalize monthly close and exception list.
- Quarterly: review runway, recurring subscriptions, tax estimates, insurance, and vendor contracts.
